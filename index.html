About me

Hi, I'm Adarsh — the person behind every jar.

I pour my heart into every single jar. No assembly lines, no shortcuts, no rushing. Just hands, soil, moss, and a deep passion for creating tiny living worlds that breathe, grow, and last.

"Every terrarium I make is a tiny living world — built to breathe, grow, and thrive on its own."

What drives me is the magic of a sealed ecosystem. Once the lid goes on, the terrarium creates its own water cycle, recycles nutrients, and sustains itself. You get a living piece of nature that essentially takes care of itself — and I find that endlessly fascinating.

I document every single build on Instagram so you can watch your terrarium come to life before it even arrives at your door. Follow along at @the_wilderness_forge.

— Adarsh, Mannheim, Germany

Care Guide

Keep your terrarium happy

Simple care instructions reduce customer questions and make your products feel more premium.

☀️ Light

Keep terrariums in bright indirect light. Avoid harsh direct sun, especially for closed jars.

💧 Water

Open moss terrariums need light watering weekly. Closed terrariums usually only need occasional misting.

🌡️ Temperature

Room temperature is ideal. Avoid radiators, cold windows, and strong drafts.

✂️ Trimming

Trim overgrown plants and remove yellow leaves to keep the ecosystem clean and balanced.
